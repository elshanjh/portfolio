About me

Hey, I’m Elshan Jabrayilzade, an email and lifecycle marketer in Tallinn who started out as a writer, and still can’t stop being one.

I plan, write and build email programmes: welcome series, automations, newsletters and campaigns, from the first email a subscriber gets to the one that asks them to buy. I work mostly in Mailchimp and Brevo, and I care as much about why someone clicks as about the numbers that show it.

Before email, I spent years in content: copywriting and social media for education brands in Baku, landing pages and reviews for affiliate sites across ten European markets, SEO, editing and a lot of poking around in analytics until things made sense. I also co-founded KYNO, a film platform, which is where most of my writing habits come from.

Outside the professional box I write stories. I do it as a kind of therapy, a way to empty my head from time to time. This site is where I put all of that: my work, my experiments, my scribbles and whatever idea refuses to leave my brain until I type it out.

I built this space mostly to have my own corner of the internet, something honest, simple and not shaped by algorithms.

If you’re here for my projects, check the Portfolio.
If you’re here to see the inside of my head, go to Stories.
