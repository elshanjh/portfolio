About me

Hey, I’m Elshan, a content guy turned storyteller who somehow ended up juggling marketing, writing and bad jokes at the same time.

I’ve worked across a bunch of areas in marketing: running affiliate websites, managing content for dozens of projects, doing SEO, writing, editing and poking around in analytics until things make sense. These days I freelance in email and lifecycle marketing, covering copy, automation and segmentation.

Outside the professional box I write stories. I do it for a kind of therapy, I am just trying to empty my head from time to time. This site is where I put all of that: my work, my experiments, my scribbles and whatever idea refuses to leave my brain until I type it out.

I built this space mostly to have my own corner of the internet, something honest, simple and not shaped by algorithms.

If you’re here for my projects, check the Portfolio.
If you’re here to see the inside of my head, go to Stories.

Feel free to look around.
