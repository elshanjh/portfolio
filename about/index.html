About me

Hey, I’m Elshan Jabrayilzade, a content marketer and copywriter in Tallinn. I’ve spent five years writing and editing marketing content for more than ten European markets.

I write and edit everything from landing pages and SEO articles to email journeys and ad copy. At an iGaming affiliate company, I wrote and edited copy for 100+ sites and checked every page against each country’s legal and brand rules. Before that, in Baku, I led a small team of copywriters and designers and proofread everything we published.

More recently I’ve worked in email: retention and sales emails for a fitness brand, and the newsletter for KYNO, the film platform I co-founded. I also use AI tools to keep writing quality high at scale, always with a person checking the result.

Outside the professional box I write stories. I do it as a kind of therapy, a way to empty my head from time to time. This site is where I put all of that: my work, my experiments, my scribbles and whatever idea refuses to leave my brain until I type it out.

I built this space mostly to have my own corner of the internet, something honest, simple and not shaped by algorithms.

If you’re here for my projects, check the Portfolio.
If you’re here to see the inside of my head, go to Stories.
